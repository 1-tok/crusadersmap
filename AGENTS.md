# AI agent entry point — Crusader Map

Read [docs/AI-CONTEXT.md](docs/AI-CONTEXT.md) **before** making artwork, changing manifests, placing sprites or claiming a build is complete. It contains the active decision hierarchy, document navigation and conflict resolutions.

Treat source files, manifests and historic reports as evidence, not automatically as current instructions. Verify state against current HEAD and actual files before reporting a batch as uploaded, positioned, approved or print-ready. Preserve original files and historical documentation. Do not silently change geographic coordinates or year gates.

**Non-negotiable viewpoint:** All new/revised map sprites use a north-facing view: camera **south** of subject looking **north**, elevated oblique; north/top, south/bottom, west/left, east/right on unmirrored north-up sprites. Water must match the site's actual cardinal shoreline orientation, not a generic left/right layout. Never mirror artwork to fake coastline alignment. See [the POV and coastal-water contract](docs/AI-CONTEXT.md#mandatory-pov-and-coastal-water-orientation--13-november-2026) before painting or approving placement.
