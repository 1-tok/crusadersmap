## Authoritative chronological scope (owner update 2026-11-15)
The master atlas spans **1096–1600 CE**: the entire Crusader era and successor Eastern Mediterranean historical landscapes through the 16th century. It is **not** an 1180-only map. Include historically attested Crusader, Byzantine, Armenian, Ayyubid, Mamluk, Ottoman, Venetian, Genoese and other relevant sites/architecture in their documented phases. The main composite is cumulative, not a claim that every depicted feature coexisted. Preserve original 1180 and other year-specific reconstruction data; enforce date gates only when generating a specifically dated view. See [active context](docs/AI-CONTEXT.md).
