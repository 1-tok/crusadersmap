# Unused sprites: what is superseded and what did not make it

1111 images in the repository ledger; 689 are on the composite.

| Group | Images | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| Superseded | 216 | a newer or preferred painting of the same site is on the map (216) |
| Reference only | 159 | sheet or master that the individual cut-outs were taken from (159) |
| Blocked | 13 | island with no coordinates anywhere in the repository (8); named site with no coordinates in the repository (4); held island already covered by a registered overlay, or with no island outline under its centre point (1) |
| Held | 8 | shows a named monument that already stands in its own city painting (5); marked repaint-required by its own file name (3) |
| Broken | 6 | file missing from the repository (3); checkerboard painted into the background (2); two pieces joined in one cut-out (1) |
| No room | 9 | site painting whose place has no room on the sheet (9) |
| Not picked | 6 | in a pool or queue but not placed in this build (6) |
| Removed | 3 | volcano or basalt piece, removed on request (3) |
| Other | 2 | replaced by the original Constantinople layout chosen by the owner (2) |

Every image is listed with its group and reason in image-usage-and-off-map-list.csv.
