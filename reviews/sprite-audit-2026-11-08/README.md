# Sprite drift audit — 8 November2026

The comparison packet reviews all21 published newly generated city sprites plus6 uncommitted draft cities. A full visual inventory covers all77 catalog records across61 distinct PNG paths. Pixel/alpha checks are technical checks only.

## Findings

- The original supplied city sprites are compact layered town masses with irregular painted detail. Many later cities repeat a circular wall, tidy tile-roof houses, a domed church and a small harbor.
- Athens and Corinth have invented water beside inland settlements. Cairo has an ungrounded water strip and generalized outer wall.
- Other sites have unresolved plans and phase evidence. Prompt exclusions are not proof that a rendered building belongs in1180.
- Recovered originals also need historical checking. The Tortosa image looks like a hill castle/bridge; two16-up alternates contain neighboring crop fragments.
- A precise-looking coordinate is a published site reference point, not evidence for the sprite footprint.

## Review standard

1. Identify the site by its documented topography and distinctive forms. Obtain a period plan or tightly bounded evidence for the particular elements shown.
2. Match the original supplied rendering style with an explicit style-only reference. Avoid borrowing its buildings, walls or heraldry.
3. Keep observed facts separate from interpretive buildings and unknown street plans. Record omissions and uncertainty.
4. Review at actual intended map size, then on light/dark backgrounds; inspect detached crop fragments as well as alpha.
5. Give each new image a review record. A completed generation, successful PNG check or researched coordinate must not create placement approval automatically.

## Release state

The unfinished fourth batch is now marked audit-hold. The importer requires an explicit published-provisional release status; folder discovery alone cannot publish new drafts. Existing published batches retain their earlier release history and approvals; this report does not certify them.

The Athens correction is a pilot outside the production sprite catalog. It requires visual acceptance and further architectural checking before release. No next batch is being generated.

## Athens evidence

- [Acropolis Restoration Service — Parthenon](https://acropolisrestoration.ysma.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/06_PARTHENWN_EN.pdf): church conversion, east apse, west entrance and12th-century repairs.
- [ASCSA — Medieval Athens](https://www.ascsa.edu.gr/publications/book/?i=9780876616079): medieval houses and the11th-century Holy Apostles church.
- [Thompson and Scranton, Hesperia](https://www.ascsa.edu.gr/uploads/media/hesperia/146915.pdf): discusses uncertainty around late12th-/early13th-century defensive repairs; it does not support the invented neat lower-town wall ring in the first image.

The correction removes the fabricated waterfront and unsupported generic lower-town enclosure. It does not establish an exact1180 settlement boundary or locate every house and church.
