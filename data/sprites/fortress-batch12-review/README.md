# Fortress Batch 12 — review candidates

This batch includes two new high-resolution source-derived fortress/city paintings: Anavarza and a **poorly matching** Kızkalesi concept. Both are available in sprite-review, not final production.

The Kızkalesi image is a wrong-sized coastal city instead of the historical offshore castle; it is registered to make the error visible for repaint. The Anavarza illustration has historically unverified tower and flag details.

`manifest.json` describes source evidence and QA. 7 of 9 planned fortresses remain unpainted. Source master artwork is in the local downloadable ZIP, with the two processed RGBA PNGs committed here.
