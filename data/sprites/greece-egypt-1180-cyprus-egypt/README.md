# Cyprus and Egypt, circa 1180

Nicosia, Kyrenia, St Hilarion, St Catherine’s, Giza and Saint Macarius at Wadi El Natrun are approved for provisional placement. The manifest connects each sprite to its numbered Crusader Period site, published coordinates, historical sources and repaint notes.

The Byzantine Cyprus depictions omit later Venetian and Lusignan structures. The Egyptian assets cover two monastic sites and the pyramid group. St Hilarion’s circa-1180 phase is inferred from the documented Byzantine setting and the 1191 reference. The precise fabric and extent need review.

The built-in image tool created the source paintings. Packaging preserved their alpha masks, cleared hidden RGB and padded the cutouts. The light and dark QA preview shows the transparent edges. The placement GeoJSON uses the main map projection for Cyprus and the Egypt and Sinai inset for the Egyptian assets.
