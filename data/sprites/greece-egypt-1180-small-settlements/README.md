# Small settlements, circa 1180

Bethlehem, Batroun and Larnaca use compact illustrations at 320 atlas pixels wide. All three are approved for provisional placement. Their manifest records the linked gazetteer sites, published placement points and historical repaint notes. The painted settlement layouts are schematic.

The built-in image tool produced the original artwork. Standard packaging preserved the alpha mask and added transparent padding.
