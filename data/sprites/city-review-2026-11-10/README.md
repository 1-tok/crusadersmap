# City sprite recovery batch: 45 paintings

45 individual PNGs committed to main in 5 regional batches. Source sheet images are retained in the downloadable archive, not yet committed to GitHub. These are review-only provisional sprites rather than historically certified artwork or final print masters.

Use `manifest.json` for provenance, QA and historical flags, and `placements-review.geojson` for all map-review points. The shared `data/sprites/placements-review-1180.geojson` includes 43 eligible candidates; Mystras and Mansoura are excluded from the 1180 view and placed in `placements-review-1250.geojson` for historically gated draft review. The word “1250” means review timeline, not a verified period reconstruction.

User authorization: every draft may be placed for visible review even without final approval. RGB edge speckles/red-yellow-cyan halos and low original source-cell resolution remain repaint/print QA tasks. Avoid duplicate sprites when the same site already has another variant.
