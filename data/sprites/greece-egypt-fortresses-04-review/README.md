# Bagras and Safed placement review

Two new fortress cutouts are ready for placement review. Bagras depicts a circa-1180 Templar phase. Safed depicts the Templar rebuilding begun in 1240, shown around 1245. Safed's art is gated out of the 1180 snapshot.

The review map displays both candidates at published geographic points and links their nameplates to the occupation and siege index. It uses the verified period map from commit e408a53. Both assets are marked pending approval. The manifest records the phase, evidence and repaint flags, including uncertainty in the reconstructed wall plans and tower forms.
