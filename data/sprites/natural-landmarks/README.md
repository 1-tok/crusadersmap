# Large natural landmark sprites

The [placement manifest](placements.geojson) uses WGS84 longitude and latitude. The [placement markup](../../../previews/natural-landmark-placement-markup.jpg) shows the geographic anchors and sprite footprints on the period map. For each approved image, `sprite_anchor_uv` identifies the named feature in the PNG as fractions measured from the upper-left corner. The builder aligns this point to the geographic anchor and uses `nominal_print_width_px` at the scale of the 10,800 px print sheet.

The paintings use an elevated view from the south looking north. The period-map builder composites placed landscapes above the terrain wash and below settlement artwork. It draws landmark names separately and reserves room for them when laying out settlement labels.

- **NAT-L018 Cedars of Lebanon** and **NAT-L019 Mount Hermon** are approved and placed on the mainland frame.
- **NAT-L017 Cappadocia** is approved for a central Anatolia extension. The current map frames end before Göreme.
- **NAT-L013–NAT-L016** retain their proposed placements and names while awaiting the original paintings.
