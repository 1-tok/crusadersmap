# Crusader fortress painting queue — real places, not generic town concepts

**Updated:** 2026-11-11. The unlabeled 12-sprite cutout sheet is concept art, not proof of identification. Historical geometry, date and heraldry must be checked before production.

## New concept batch Q13 (12 candidate identities; PNG import pending)

1. **Chastellet / Jacob's Ford** — unfinished 1178–1179 frontier fortress immediately beside the Jordan; show partly built defenses, not a mature hilltop castle. **Never show intact in 1180.**
2. **Bourzey (Qal'at Burzey)** — prominent western Syrian rocky spur, irregular masonry adapted to ridge; avoid generic tiered village.
3. **Shaizar** — exceptionally long, narrow citadel on the Orontes escarpment; no generic hilltop circular enceinte.
4. **Masyaf** — ridge-dominating Isma'ili castle; **do not put Frankish red-cross flags** on its towers.
5. **Qadmus** — compact mountain ridge stronghold in the Syrian coastal range; verify Isma'ili period and historical masonry.
6. **Darbsak (Trapessac)** — steep northern Syrian/Cilician frontier ridge, fortified summit; verify control period and avoid invented river bridge.
7. **Feke / Vahka** — Armenian Cilician mountain fortification, not a generic Latin crusader castle; match its elongated ridge.
8. **Kızkalesi / Maiden's Castle** — **small offshore island fort** in the Cilician Mediterranean, NOT a mainland fortified harbor or large city; geometry must closely match the island. Later date/fabric review required.
9. **Buffavento** — very high Kyrenia mountain ridge castle above northern Cyprus; buildings extend discontinuously along the crest, not a compact single keep.
10. **Kantara Castle** — exposed eastern Kyrenia rocky ridge castle with discontinuous hilltop walls, not a waterfront fort.
11. **Kolossi** — later square tower/commandery west of Limassol; review the surviving **15th-century** keep separately from any 12th-century site use. No present-day tower in a strict 1180 depiction.
12. **Chlemoutsi / Clermont** — Frankish castle in the western Peloponnese, begun c. 1220s; trace its irregular polygonal enclosure, **exclude from 1180**.

## Existing paintings and exclusions

- The 45 city review PNGs from November 10 are already on main; **do not repaint merely because the old November 10 missing-city manifest says they are unpainted**.
- Anavarza (provisional) and Kızkalesi (wrong coastal city concept) already exist as batch12 drawings. Kızkalesi Q13 is a *new concept* to compare; it does not silently replace the original variant.
- Bagras and Safed have review paintings. Safed's mid-13th-century rebuilt fortress must not be used as an 1180 reconstruction.
- For every new painting: south-facing elevated oblique view, geographically appropriate shore/ridge/river, isolated transparent cutout, no modern roads or structures, native-resolution QA, and no unverified Frankish banner on Muslim/Armenian/Byzantine sites.
