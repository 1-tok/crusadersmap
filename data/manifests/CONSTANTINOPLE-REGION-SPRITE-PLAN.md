# Constantinople companion sprites — 1180

Constantinople remains approved for provisional placement at double size and is explicitly flagged for future replacement. The replacement must use a documented city plan and recheck monument positions, palace condition, walls and coastline.

| Priority | Candidate | Placement concept | Historical constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Galata / Sykai (Pera) | North bank of Golden Horn; small separate companion | Byzantine waterfront chain fort, not the1348 Genoese hilltop tower |
| 2 | Chrysopolis / Skoutarion (Üsküdar) | Asian shore, northeast across the Bosphorus | Verify1180 settlement and landing; exclude Ottoman skyline |
| 3 | Chalcedon / Chalkēdōn (Kadıköy) | Asian shore, southeast of Chrysopolis | Verify medieval extent rather than reproduce the ancient city |

Start with companions at a proposed256 atlas pixels versus Constantinople1024. At the existing regional map scale they may collide or cover the strait; review an enlarged local inset before committing to artwork sizes. Preserve true anchors and use documented visual offsets/leader lines when necessary. Blachernae belongs inside the central city composition, not a duplicate city sprite.

The machine-readable plan stores research links, uncertainty and null coordinates until verified. These are research candidates, not approved assets. Galata/Sykai has direct medieval settlement evidence in [Dalleggio d’Alessio](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rebyz_0766-5598_1961_num_19_1_1265). The later tower date is documented by the [Ministry of Culture museum leaflet](https://muze.gov.tr/s3/MysFileLibrary/566f7fe8-c812-421d-9baa-86e7bb228337.pdf). Üsküdar and Kadıköy municipal source leads still need deeper1180-specific review.
